Problem Statement¶

Business Context¶

The healthcare industry is rapidly evolving, with professionals facing increasing challenges in managing vast volumes of medical data while delivering accurate and timely diagnoses. Quick access to comprehensive, reliable, and up-to-date medical knowledge is critical for improving patient outcomes and ensuring informed decision-making in a fast-paced environment.

Healthcare professionals often encounter information overload, struggling to sift through extensive research and data to create accurate diagnoses and treatment plans. This challenge is amplified by the need for efficiency, particularly in emergencies, where time-sensitive decisions are vital. Furthermore, access to trusted, current medical information from renowned manuals and research papers is essential for maintaining high standards of care.

Common Questions to Answer

1. What is the protocol for managing sepsis in a critical care unit?

2. What are the common symptoms of appendicitis, and can it be cured via medicine? If not, what surgical procedure should be followed to treat it?

3. What are the effective treatments or solutions for addressing sudden patchy hair loss, commonly seen as localized bald spots on the scalp, and what could be the possible causes behind it?

4. What treatments are recommended for a person who has sustained a physical injury to brain tissue, resulting in temporary or permanent impairment of brain function?

5. What are the necessary precautions and treatment steps for a person who has fractured their leg during a hiking trip, and what should be considered for their care and recovery?

Objective¶

As an AI specialist, your task is to develop a RAG-based AI solution using renowned medical manuals to address healthcare challenges. The objective is to understand information overload, apply AI techniques to streamline decision-making, analyze its impact on diagnostics and patient outcomes, evaluate its potential to standardize care practices, and create a functional prototype demonstrating its feasibility and effectiveness.

Data Description¶

The Merck Manuals are medical references published by the American pharmaceutical company Merck & Co., that cover a wide range of medical topics, including disorders, tests, diagnoses, and drugs. The manuals have been published since 1899.

The manual is a PDF with over 4,000 pages divided into 23 sections.

def response(query, max_tokens=128, temperature=0, top_p=0.95, top_k=50):
    """
    Generate a response from the LLM for a given query.
    
    Parameters:
    -----------
    query       : str   – The input question / prompt
    max_tokens  : int   – Maximum number of tokens to generate
    temperature : float – Randomness (0 = deterministic, 1 = creative)
    top_p       : float – Nucleus sampling probability threshold
    top_k       : int   – Top-k sampling (limits token selection pool)
    
    Returns:
    --------
    str : The model's generated text response
    """
    model_output = llm(
        prompt=query,
        max_tokens=max_tokens,
        temperature=temperature,
        top_p=top_p,
        top_k=top_k
    )
    return model_output['choices'][0]['text']

Response Function¶

In [57]:
def generate_rag_response(user_input, k=5, max_tokens=256, temperature=0, top_p=0.95, top_k=50):
    """
    Generate a RAG-enhanced response by retrieving relevant Merck Manual passages
    and providing them as context to the LLM.
    
    Parameters:
    -----------
    user_input  : str   – Medical question to answer
    k           : int   – Number of document chunks to retrieve
    max_tokens  : int   – Maximum tokens in response
    temperature : float – Response randomness (0 = deterministic)
    top_p       : float – Nucleus sampling probability
    top_k       : int   – Top-k token sampling
    
    Returns:
    --------
    str : RAG-enhanced medical response grounded in the Merck Manual
    """
    global qna_system_message, qna_user_message_template
    
    # Step 1: Retrieve relevant chunks from ChromaDB
    relevant_document_chunks = retriever.get_relevant_documents(query=user_input, k=k)
    context_list = [d.page_content for d in relevant_document_chunks]
    
    # Step 2: Combine chunks into context string
    context_for_query = ". ".join(context_list)
    
    # Step 3: Build the prompt with context and question
    user_message = qna_user_message_template.replace('{context}', context_for_query)
    user_message = user_message.replace('{question}', user_input)
    prompt = qna_system_message + '\n' + user_message
    
    # Step 4: Generate response
    try:
        result = llm(
            prompt=prompt,
            max_tokens=max_tokens,
            temperature=temperature,
            top_p=top_p,
            top_k=top_k
        )
        response_text = result['choices'][0]['text'].strip()
    except Exception as e:
        response_text = f'Error generating response: {e}'
    
    return response_text

def generate_ground_relevance_response(user_input, k=5, max_tokens=256, temperature=0, top_p=0.95, top_k=50):
    """
    Evaluate groundedness and relevance of the RAG response for a given query.
    Uses LLM-as-a-Judge approach with separate evaluation prompts.
    
    Returns:
    --------
    tuple: (groundedness_score_text, relevance_score_text)
    """
    global qna_system_message, qna_user_message_template

    # Step 1: Retrieve context
    relevant_document_chunks = retriever.get_relevant_documents(query=user_input, k=k)
    context_list = [d.page_content for d in relevant_document_chunks]
    context_for_query = ". ".join(context_list)

    # Step 2: Generate the RAG answer to evaluate
    user_message = qna_user_message_template.replace('{context}', context_for_query)
    user_message = user_message.replace('{question}', user_input)
    prompt = qna_system_message + '\n' + user_message

    answer_result = llm(prompt=prompt, max_tokens=max_tokens, temperature=temperature,
                        top_p=top_p, top_k=top_k)
    answer = answer_result['choices'][0]['text'].strip()

    # Step 3: Build evaluation prompts
    eval_context = context_for_query[:1000]  # truncate for eval context
    eval_user_msg = user_message_template.format(
        context=eval_context, question=user_input, answer=answer
    )

    groundedness_prompt = groundedness_rater_system_message + "\n" + eval_user_msg
    relevance_prompt = relevance_rater_system_message + "\n" + eval_user_msg

    # Step 4: Get groundedness score
    g_result = llm(prompt=groundedness_prompt, max_tokens=100, temperature=0,
                   top_p=0.95, top_k=50)
    groundedness_score = g_result['choices'][0]['text'].strip()

    # Step 5: Get relevance score
    r_result = llm(prompt=relevance_prompt, max_tokens=100, temperature=0,
                   top_p=0.95, top_k=50)
    relevance_score = r_result['choices'][0]['text'].strip()

    print(f"RAG Answer:\n{answer}")
    print(f"\nGroundedness: {groundedness_score}")
    print(f"Relevance:    {relevance_score}")

    return groundedness_score, relevance_score

💡 Business Recommendations for Healthcare Centers¶

1. Deploy RAG as a Clinical Decision Support Tool The system demonstrates high groundedness in authoritative medical literature. Deploying it as a decision support tool (not a replacement for physicians) can reduce the time clinicians spend searching through medical references — particularly valuable in emergency settings where time is critical.

2. Use Chunking Strategy Carefully From fine-tuning experiments, chunk_size=400–500 with k=5–7 retrieval provides the best balance of context specificity and recall completeness. Overly large chunks reduce retrieval precision; too small chunks fragment clinical protocols across too many pieces.

3. Prompt Engineering Improves Response Quality Significantly The structured clinical prompt (Combination 2 — numbered steps with explicit structure) consistently produced the most actionable clinical responses. Healthcare organizations should invest in prompt engineering with clinical domain experts to design system prompts optimized for their specific use cases (ICU, emergency, primary care).

4. Implement Continuous Evaluation with LLM-as-Judge The groundedness/relevance evaluation framework demonstrated that automated quality scoring is feasible. Deploy this as a monitoring layer in production to flag responses below quality thresholds for human review before delivery to clinicians.

5. Expand the Knowledge Base Iteratively The Merck Manual covers broad internal medicine topics. Specialty-specific RAG systems (oncology protocols, cardiology guidelines, pediatric dosing references) would provide even higher relevance scores for domain-specific queries. A modular, multi-source RAG architecture would allow healthcare centers to route queries to the most appropriate knowledge base.

6. Address Hallucination Risk with Strict Grounding The system prompt's explicit instruction — "answer ONLY using the provided context" — is essential in healthcare settings where hallucinated information could directly harm patients. Strict grounding with groundedness evaluation scores below 4/5 should trigger a fallback to human expert review.
